# Harbourline Retail privacy and data handling policy

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional policy for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail. It is not legal advice.

## Customer data

Harbourline collects only the personal information needed to run the Harbourline Rewards program, process online orders, and meet legal obligations. Customer data is stored in Australia. Loyalty transaction history is kept for 7 years and then deleted or de-identified.

## CCTV

In-store CCTV footage is retained for 30 days and then overwritten, unless it is needed for an incident investigation, in which case the relevant footage is kept until the investigation closes. Only the store manager and the loss prevention team may view footage.

## Access requests

A customer may ask for a copy of the personal information Harbourline holds about them. Harbourline responds to access requests within 30 days, free of charge.

## Data breaches

A suspected data breach must be reported to the privacy officer within 1 hour of discovery. The privacy officer assesses within 72 hours whether the breach is likely to cause serious harm and must be notified to affected customers and the regulator.

## Use of AI assistants

Team members may use approved AI assistants for internal documents and data analysis. Customer personal information must never be entered into an AI assistant that has not been approved by the privacy officer, and AI output that affects a customer (such as a refund decision) must be reviewed by a person.
