# Harbourline Retail supplier code of conduct

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional supplier code for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail.

## Payment terms

Harbourline pays small suppliers (annual turnover below A$10 million) within 20 days of a correct invoice. All other suppliers are paid within 45 days. Disputed invoices are acknowledged within 5 business days.

## Food safety

Suppliers of fresh, chilled, and frozen food must hold a current GFSI-recognised food safety certification and must notify Harbourline's quality team within 4 hours of discovering a food safety issue that could lead to a recall.

## Ethical sourcing

Suppliers must not use forced, bonded, or child labour anywhere in their supply chain. Harbourline may audit any supplier site with 48 hours' notice; high-risk sites identified in the annual modern slavery risk assessment are audited at least every 2 years by an independent auditor.

## Packaging

From FY26, all own-brand packaging must be recyclable, reusable, or compostable. Suppliers must report the weight of packaging they supply to Harbourline twice a year.

## Gifts and hospitality

Harbourline team members may not accept gifts from suppliers worth more than A$100. Any gift or hospitality offer, accepted or declined, is recorded in the gifts register within 7 days.
