# Harbourline Rewards program terms (summary)

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — Harbourline Rewards is a fictional loyalty program invented for this demo. It is not based on any real Australian loyalty scheme.

## Membership

Membership is free and open to Australian residents aged 16 or over. Each member may hold one active account. Accounts with no earning or redemption activity for 18 consecutive months are closed and any unredeemed points are forfeited.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point for every A$1 spent on eligible purchases, rounded down to the nearest dollar. Gift cards, tobacco products, lottery products, and delivery fees are not eligible purchases and do not earn points.

Bonus point offers are sent to members by email or the app. A bonus offer must be activated before the qualifying purchase is made; bonus points cannot be applied retrospectively.

## Membership tiers

There are three tiers: Member, Silver, and Gold.

- Member is the default tier on joining.
- Silver requires A$3,000 of eligible spend in a membership year and earns 1.25 points per A$1.
- Gold requires A$6,000 of eligible spend in a membership year and earns 1.5 points per A$1, plus free standard home delivery on orders over A$50.

Tier status is reviewed on the anniversary of the member's joining date.

## Redeeming points

Points are redeemed in blocks of 2,000 points for a A$10 discount at checkout. A maximum of A$100 of discount can be redeemed in a single transaction.

Points expire 24 months after the month in which they were earned.

## Privacy and data use

Harbourline uses purchase history to personalise offers. Members can opt out of personalised offers at any time in the app without leaving the program. Harbourline does not sell member personal information to third parties. Requests to access or correct personal information are handled by the privacy team within 30 days.
