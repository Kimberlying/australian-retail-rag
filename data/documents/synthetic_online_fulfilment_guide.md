# Harbourline Retail online order fulfilment guide

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional guide for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail.

## Home delivery

Home delivery is available from metropolitan stores in two-hour windows between 7:00 am and 9:00 pm. The standard delivery fee is A$9, reduced to A$4 for orders over A$150. Gold tier Harbourline Rewards members get free standard delivery on orders over A$50.

## Substitutions

Customers choose whether to allow substitutions when they order. A substitute must be the same product type and size or larger, at the same price or lower. If a substitute costs more, the customer pays the original price. Customers may reject a substitute at the door for a full refund of that item.

## Picking quality

Pickers choose fresh produce with at least 3 days of shelf life remaining, and chilled items with at least 5 days where available. Chilled and frozen items are picked last and packed in insulated bags with ice packs.

## Failed deliveries

If no one is home, the driver calls the customer and waits up to 10 minutes. Undelivered chilled and frozen items are not redelivered; they are written off and refunded. Ambient items are returned to the store and the customer may rebook delivery within 48 hours at no extra charge.

## Order cut-off

Orders can be changed until 10:00 pm the night before the delivery day. Same-day delivery orders must be placed before 12:00 pm.
