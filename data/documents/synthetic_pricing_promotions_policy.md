# Harbourline Retail pricing and promotions policy

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional pricing policy for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail. It does not describe consumer law requirements.

## Shelf labels

Every product on display must have a shelf label showing the price and the unit price (for example, price per 100 g or per litre). Promotional tickets are yellow and must show the promotion end date. Price changes are loaded overnight and shelf labels are updated before 8:00 am on the change date.

## Scanning errors

If a product scans at the register above the shelf price, the customer is charged the lower shelf price. If the item costs A$20 or less, the first item is given to the customer free of charge; items over A$20 are sold at the lower price. Every scanning error is logged so the pricing team can fix the master price within 24 hours.

## Weekly promotions

The promotional week runs from Wednesday to the following Tuesday. Catalogue promotions are planned 10 weeks in advance. A product may be on promotion for no more than 4 weeks in any 13-week period, so that "was" prices remain genuine.

## Markdowns for short-dated stock

Fresh products with one day of shelf life remaining are marked down by 30%. On the final day of shelf life, the markdown increases to 50% after 4:00 pm. Markdown labels are orange and must never cover the use-by date.

## Price matching

Harbourline does not price match competitors. Store managers may not change a shelf price locally except to correct an error, and any local correction must be reported to the pricing team the same day.
