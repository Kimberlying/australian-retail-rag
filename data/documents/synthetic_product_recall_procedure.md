# Harbourline Retail product recall and withdrawal procedure

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional internal procedure for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail. It is not legal advice and does not describe any regulator's actual requirements.

## Recall versus withdrawal

A **recall** removes unsafe product from sale and from consumers, because the product may cause illness or injury. A **withdrawal** removes product from sale for a non-safety reason, such as a labelling error that does not affect safety or a quality defect.

## Severity classes

- **Class A (critical):** a risk of serious illness, injury, or death — for example an undeclared allergen or a foreign object such as glass or metal.
- **Class B (moderate):** a risk of minor, reversible harm.
- **Class C (quality):** no safety risk; handled as a withdrawal.

## Timelines

For a Class A recall, affected stock must be removed from shelves in every store within 2 hours of the recall notice. A point-of-sale block must be applied to the product barcode within 30 minutes so that it cannot be scanned at any checkout.

For a Class B recall, stock must be removed within 24 hours. Class C withdrawals must be completed within 72 hours.

## Customer notification

For Class A and Class B recalls, a notice is displayed at store entrances and on the Harbourline website for at least 30 days. Harbourline Rewards members who purchased the affected product are notified directly by email and app notification using their purchase history.

Customers returning recalled product receive a full refund without needing a receipt.

## Roles

The recall coordinator in the head office quality team owns the decision to recall and the severity classification. Store managers are responsible for shelf removal and confirming completion in the recall register. Store staff must not decide on their own that a product is safe to return to sale.

## Recall register

Every store must confirm completion in the recall register with the time of removal and the quantity removed. A recall is closed only when all stores have confirmed and the stock reconciliation variance is below 2% of the expected quantity.
