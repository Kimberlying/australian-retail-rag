# Harbourline Retail store operations handbook

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — Harbourline Retail is a fictional company. Nothing in this document describes a real retailer's procedures.

**Version:** 2.3 (synthetic)

## Store opening checklist

The duty manager must arrive at least 45 minutes before trading starts. Before doors open, the duty manager confirms that all refrigerated display cases have logged a temperature within range for the previous two hours, that the self-service checkouts have completed their start-of-day diagnostics, and that the first-aid kit seal is intact.

If any refrigerated case has a temperature breach overnight, the duty manager must quarantine the affected stock and raise a cold chain incident before opening.

## Cold chain temperature limits

Chilled products (dairy, fresh meat, ready meals) must be held between 0°C and 5°C. Frozen products must be held at or below -18°C. A reading above 5°C for chilled stock for more than 30 minutes is classified as a cold chain breach.

Stock involved in a cold chain breach cannot be sold, marked down, or donated. It must be recorded in the waste log with the reason code COLD_CHAIN and disposed of the same day.

## Click and collect service levels

Standard click and collect orders must be picked and staged within 2 hours of the order being released to the store. Orders placed before 11:00 am are promised for same-day collection. Orders placed after 11:00 am are promised for next-day collection.

Staged orders are held for a maximum of 3 days. On the fourth day, uncollected orders are cancelled, the customer is refunded in full, and the ambient items are returned to the shelf. Chilled and frozen items from uncollected orders are written off.

## Shrink and loss reporting

Shrink is measured as the value of unexplained stock loss as a percentage of sales. Each store reviews its shrink report every Monday. A store whose weekly shrink exceeds 1.8% of sales for three consecutive weeks is escalated to the regional loss prevention manager.

Staff must never confront a suspected shoplifter. The correct procedure is to observe, record the time and description, and notify the duty manager.

## Customer incident reporting

Any customer injury on premises, however minor, must be entered into the incident register within 24 hours. The incident record must include the time, location in store, a description, and the names of staff who attended. Photographs of the location should be taken where it is safe to do so.
