# Harbourline Retail workplace health and safety procedure

**Document type:** Synthetic portfolio data — a fictional procedure for the fictional retailer Harbourline Retail. It is not legal or work health and safety advice.

## Manual handling

Team members must not lift a single item heavier than 20 kg without a mechanical aid or a second person. Cartons of bottled water, pet food, and laundry powder are the most common manual handling injuries and must be moved with a trolley. Every team member completes manual handling refresher training every 12 months.

## Powered equipment

Only team members holding a current high-risk work licence may operate a forklift. Electric pallet jacks may be used by any team member after a supervised induction of at least two shifts. Pallet jacks must never be used on the shop floor while customers are within 3 metres.

## Spills and trip hazards

A spill must be guarded immediately with a yellow wet-floor sign and cleaned within 5 minutes of being reported. Floor walks are recorded every 60 minutes during trading hours on the store safety log.

## Incidents and first aid

Every store must have at least one team member with a current first aid certificate rostered on each shift. Staff injuries are reported to the store manager before the end of the shift and entered in the incident register within 24 hours. A notifiable incident, such as a serious injury requiring hospital admission, is escalated to the state safety manager within 1 hour.

## Heat and cold stores

Team members may work in the freezer room for no more than 20 minutes at a time and must wear the supplied freezer jacket and gloves. On days forecast above 35°C, loading dock work is rotated every 30 minutes.
